## 1. Category Selection and Rationale

First category: **robot vacuum cleaners** (consumer appliance). Rationale: ① clear entity tiers — 7 head brands plus long tail, so mention rates are discriminating; ② rich review ecosystems in both Chinese and English, so citation share has content to count; ③ shared entities with M1's sample E2 (Roborock) for cross-module corroboration; ④ explicit B2C purchase intent, where the "answer-as-decision" displacement effect is most typical. A second category may copy this structure in a later quarter (candidates: air purifiers, smart speakers).

## 4. Execution Protocol

| Item | First baseline (2026-09) | Later monthly rounds |
|---|---|---|
| Question scope | Core 30: A-layer 6 (A01/A02/A05/A13/A14/A15) + B-layer 15 of 25 + C-layer 5 (C01/C02/C05/C09/C11) | Expand to full 60 |
| Platforms | ChatGPT, Perplexity, Doubao (3) | +Gemini, AI Overviews, Yuanbao, Kimi (7 entries) |
| Variance sampling | 6 fixed questions (A01, B01, B02, C01, C09, C14) × 3 runs per platform | Same |
| Frequency | Within 3 days around the 1st of each month | Same |
| Per-round workload | 30×3 + 6×3×2 (repeats) = 126 answers | Full 60×7 |

Execution environment recorded: platform, mode (web on by default), date, visible model version.

## 5. Statistical Definitions

| Metric | Definition | Calculation |
|---|---|---|
| Mention rate | Share of questions mentioning entity Tn (by layer) | Questions mentioning / executed questions in layer |
| Citation share | Entity-affiliated domain citations / all citations for the question | Domain attribution table built first (official sites / flagship stores / vertical review sites) |
| Citation domain distribution | Frequency table of all cited domains | Top10 concentration + long tail |
| Answer position | Position of the entity: first recommendation / mixed / mentioned only | Tiered counts |
| Citation absorption | Depth to which entity parameters (price/spec/selling points) enter the answer body: concrete parameters > brand name only | Sampled on B/C layers |
| Stability | Cross-run consistency of the 6 variance-sampled questions (mean Jaccard of mention sets and citation sets) | Reported per platform |

Methodological basis: single-run AI-visibility results are sample estimates and require repeated execution with confidence intervals (arxiv 2603.08924 [2026-09][B]); and the two-stage "selection → absorption" scoring (arxiv 2604.25707 [2026-09][A]).
