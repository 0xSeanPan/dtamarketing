## Gate Criteria (Phase 0 → Phase 1)

- M1: first-round recognition-accuracy data for three sample entities; the method is reproducible (another person executes it with identical results)
- M2: citation-share distribution for one category across at least 3 platforms
- Monitoring source list completes its first sweep: dead sources removed, "to verify" items resolved

## Risks and Responses

| Risk | Impact | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Platforms and protocols shift quickly; conclusions have a short shelf life | Research depreciates | Date-stamp every conclusion; monthly retest cadence; recalibrate the roadmap quarterly |
| Chinese-market behavior deviates from English-language research | Direct transplants fail | M2 carries dedicated Chinese-language questions (RQ5); domestic models join the panel |
| Sample entities carry too little traffic for statistical significance | Conclusions unreliable | Prefer entities with organic traffic; downgrade to qualitative conclusions and label them when samples are thin |
| Research sprawl crowds out execution | Research without a business | Phase 3 has hard deliverables: an opportunity trade-off list and a skill-investment list |
