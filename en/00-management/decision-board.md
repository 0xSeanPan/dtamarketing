## Decision Record Format

```
### Dn decision title
- Question:
- Options and conclusion sources: (cite module-document conclusions with evidence grades)
- Decision:
- Resources committed / released:
- Effective date and review date:
```

## Trade-off Rules (linked to `../03-commercialization/opportunity-matrix.md`)

- Total score below threshold, or "demand authenticity" at 1: drop; record the reason; do not re-review unless new evidence appears
- Low "timing maturity" but high "personal fit": convert to a watchlist item, add to monitoring sources, sweep quarterly
- When resources (time/money) conflict, prefer options that produce reusable assets (methods, data, content assets over one-off deliverables)
